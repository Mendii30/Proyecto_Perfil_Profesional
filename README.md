🍱 Project 1: Gestía Alimentos — Food Service & Stock Management
Gestía Alimentos is a system I built for school kitchens. It helps cooks manage their food stock and plan their daily menus easily.

The Problem: School kitchens often have problems with missing ingredients, food waste, and difficult manual calculations.
Key Features:
Dietas Especiales (Special Diets): A section to manage food for students with allergies or special needs, like celiac disease.
Stock Tracking: The system updates the food inventory automatically when meals are made.
Alerts: It warns the cook if there is not enough food to prepare a menu.
Methodology: I used the PHVA (Plan-Do-Check-Act) cycle to test and improve the application.
Tech Stack: Python, Streamlit, SQL, Docker.
